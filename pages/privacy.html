Privacy Policy

At NaijaBiz, we respect your data and transparency is our priority.

Information Collection

We collect business details (name, category, WhatsApp) which are intentionally made public for marketing. We collect your email for account security and payment receipts via Paystack.

Payment Security

We do not store credit card information. All payments are handled by Paystack, a PCI-DSS certified payment processor. They transmit your data securely to finalize verification subscriptions.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We only share information with our technical partners (Firebase & Paystack) necessary to run the service.
