🛡️ Smart Shopping Guide

Most vendors require payment before shipping. Here is how to ensure you are dealing with a legit business before you transfer money.

Rule #1: The TikTok Audit (Do this FIRST)

Before you pay a kobo, click the TikTok link on their profile and check these 3 things:

  • Are they active? Check if they posted recently. Dormant accounts are risky.
  • Are the products real? Look for videos where they actually hold or use the product. Avoid accounts that only post generic pictures.
  • Check the Comments: Read what others are saying. If you see complaints about "scam" or "no delivery," run away.

2. Pickup Safety (Public Places Only)

If the vendor is in your city and allows pickup, never go to a private house or a secluded area. Always insist on meeting in a busy public place like:

  • A popular Eatery or Fast Food joint.
  • Inside a Shopping Mall.
  • A busy Bus Terminal.

3. Inspect Upon Delivery

Once the rider delivers your package (or you pick it up):

  • Open it immediately. Don't wait until you get home.
  • Check for damage or wrong items.
  • Report Issues Instantly: If something is wrong, take a picture and message the vendor immediately on WhatsApp. If they refuse to solve it, report them to us.

4. Trust the Verified Badge

Vendors with the ✔ Blue Tick have submitted their ID/CAC documents to us. They are generally safer to trade with.
