Frequently Asked Questions

Is NaijaBiz free to use?

Yes! Buyers can browse for free. Vendors can list their business for free on the Standard plan.

How do I verify my business?

Currently, verification is done manually. After you register, upload a clear TikTok video of your product. Our team reviews all new listings within 24 hours.

A vendor scammed me. What do I do?

Please report them immediately using the Report a Vendor page. We will investigate and ban their account if found guilty.

How does the WhatsApp chat work?

When you click "Chat on WhatsApp," we open a chat with the seller and automatically paste a message saying you came from NaijaBiz.
