Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify my business? +
Log in to your Dashboard, go to "Brand Identity," and choose a verification tier. Payments are processed securely via Paystack and your badge is activated instantly.
What is the difference between Blue and Gold? +
Blue Verification gives you a trust badge. Gold Premium gives you the badge PLUS a cinematic profile header and priority placement in search results.
How do buyers contact me? +
When you add your WhatsApp number in the dashboard, we create a direct "Order on WhatsApp" button on your profile for customers.
Is NaijaBiz involved in the payment for goods? +
No. We are a directory. All product payments happen directly between you and the buyer on WhatsApp. We only handle verification fees.
